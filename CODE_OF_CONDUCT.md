# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The SyntraLang project aims to provide a welcoming, respectful environment for everyone who participates, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith while evaluating technical claims critically.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Welcome questions and beginner contributions.
- Give actionable feedback about code, not personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruptive conduct.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberate trolling or repeated bad-faith disruption.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports should be made privately through the maintainer's GitHub contact route. Include relevant links and a concise description; avoid reposting sensitive information publicly.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code review, and project-related community interactions.
